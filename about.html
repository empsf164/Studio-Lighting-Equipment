Our Philosophy & Heritage

SHAPE LIGHT. CREATE WITH PRECISION.

Lumora Studio was founded by cinematographers, optical engineers, and studio gaffers who believe that professional lighting is an exact science as much as an art form.

Engineering Tenets

The Four Quality Pillars

Spectral Purity

Guaranteeing 98+ CRI and continuous R9 saturated red rendition so human skin tones look rich and organic directly in-camera.

Optical Modularity

Universal Bowens S-mount integration ensuring rapid swapping of parabolic softboxes, Fresnel condensers, and optical snoots.

Silent Thermal Control

Vapor-chamber cooling profiles operating under 26dB so that sound recordists capture pristine dialogue on quiet interview sets.

Studio Reliability

Aviation-grade aluminum unibody housings built to endure demanding commercial production sets, trucks, and location shoots.

Spectrophotometric Validation

Rigorous Lab Calibration

Every batch of LED chips is sampled inside our dark-chamber integrating sphere to verify chromaticity coordinates (duv < 0.002) and color stability across the entire 0.1% to 100% dimming spectrum.

We refuse to cut corners on passive heatsinks or optical glass. The result is lighting you can trust on multimillion-dollar productions with zero green/magenta shift.
