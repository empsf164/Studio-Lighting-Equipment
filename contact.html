Frequently Asked Questions

Are Lumora lights compatible with standard Bowens modifiers?

Yes. All Lumora point-source COB lights and strobe heads feature standard Bowens S-mount precision alloy bayonets.

Can Lumora COB lights run on V-Mount batteries on location?

Yes. The X600 and Beam 300 support dual 14.4V / 26V high-draw V-mount battery inputs with active voltage regulation.

Do you provide high-speed sync support for strobes?

Yes. The Strobe 800 supports TTL auto-metering and HSS shutter speeds up to 1/8000s across Sony, Canon, Nikon, and Fuji systems.
